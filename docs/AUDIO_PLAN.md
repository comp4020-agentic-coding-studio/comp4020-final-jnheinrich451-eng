# Audio stage — planned

The author intends to generate background music with Gemini. No music asset
has been supplied or integrated yet. Sound effects are a separate stage.

Keep music and effects volumes independent, with mute controls. Start playback
after a player gesture, pause on hidden tabs, and avoid restarting a track on
each game-state update. Prefer instrumental loops and restrained music beneath
weapon and warning cues. Playback belongs in the browser, not the game simulation.

Before adding a track to the public repository, record its generator/model,
generation date, prompt, original filename, any edits and the applicable terms.
Credit it accurately as AI-generated background music. Prefer original mood,
instrument and tempo descriptions; avoid copying melodies, lyrics or recordings.

Google's current Gemini help permits downloading and sharing generated tracks.
Its general terms say Google does not claim ownership over generated original
content, but require respecting third-party rights. These statements are not
a guarantee of non-infringement or exclusive copyright in an output, nor a
specific commercial game licence. Check any additional terms attached to the
actual tool/account before publication; Gemini Apps, APIs and experimental music
tools should not be treated as interchangeable.
