Enemy sketches v1, 9 November 2026. Review material only; not wired into the game.

Source: the seven concept renders in this folder (hunter v1, breaker v2, berserker v2,
twin laser v1, heavy laser v1, siege centipede v1, swimmer v1), simplified by hand into
the game's flat Canvas style. No image generator was used for the sketches.

Files
- enemy-sketches.js: one draw function per creature, in the game's convention
  (pixels at zoom 1, 48 px per tile, origin at the body centre, front toward -y).
- render-sketches.mjs: renders the review sheets (run from the repo root:
  node output/enemy-review/render-sketches.mjs).
- sketch-sheet-v1.png: each concept beside its sketch, with the features kept.
- sketch-battlefield-v1.png: current art and the sketches at zoom 1 on Caldera, Vesper
  and Sirocco ground, plus Caldera at zoom 0.6, with the robot and hit circles for scale.

Assumptions and open questions for the author
- Berserker is drawn as the replacement for the Bulwark drawing. The in-game kind and
  its labels (BULWARK / HE + MAG) are unchanged.
- The Heavy laser is shown at 2.5 times the Twin laser's width, as requested. All
  crawlers share a 0.38-tile hit circle, so at that size most of the visible body
  (barrel, legs) would not take hits. Keeping the art near today's size, or enlarging
  the hit circle (a server and balance change), is the author's decision.
- The Hunter changes from six legs to four legs and two clamps, as in the concept.
  Presentation only.
- The centipede's flower maw reaches about 0.75 tile past the head's collision circle;
  today's mandibles already reach past it. Plates stay inside the eight circles.
- Slate Berserker on Caldera's blue meadow is the weakest contrast of the set; it still
  reads by outline and shadow.

Not yet sketched: dead, burning/fleeing and hit-flash states beyond a simple hit colour.
Barrels on both laser classes sit in a group that turns about the neck, so aim can stay
independent of the body as in drawLensCrawler.
