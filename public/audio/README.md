# Runtime music

`Apex_Of_Conquest.mp3` is an unchanged copy of the author's supplied
`audio/Apex_Of_Conquest.mp3`, generated using Gemini according to the author.
The author requested its inclusion as this project's background music on
23 October 2026. The author reports permission to use it in their project;
this note does not claim exclusive copyright or independent licence verification.

On 9 November 2026 the author assigned Crimson Vanguard to Sirocco and Steel
Wrought Ascent to Vesper, leaving Apex of Conquest on Caldera. The author also
requested one of the three supplied colonization tracks for each peaceful
planet and left those pairings to Codex:

| Mode | Planet | Runtime file (unchanged from `audio/`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| War Zone | Caldera | `Apex_Of_Conquest.mp3` |
| War Zone | Sirocco | `Crimson_Vanguard.mp3` |
| War Zone | Vesper | `Steel_Wrought_Ascent.mp3` |
| Colonization | Morrow | `Twilight_Over_the_Still_Lake_Colonization.mp3` |
| Colonization | Nacre | `Harvest_at_Low_Tide_Colonnization.mp3` |
| Colonization | Aurelia | `Sun_Drenched_Sanctuary_Colonization.mp3` |

All six public files are byte-for-byte copies of the supplied MP3s; no trimming,
normalization or re-encoding was applied. The spelling `Colonnization` in the
Harvest filename is retained from the source. The author credits Gemini for the
music in `PROCESS.md`. Exact model versions, generation dates, prompts and
track-specific terms for the five newly assigned files were not supplied; this
record does not infer them or extend the earlier Apex permission statement.

The browser selects one looping track from the saved world's planet, with a
shared saved music toggle/volume independent of sound effects. No external audio
service is called. See [the audio stage record](../../docs/AUDIO_PLAN.md).
