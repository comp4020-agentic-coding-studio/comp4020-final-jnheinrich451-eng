FIELD GUIDE

Your first foothold.

A shared battlefield. Begin in a rover, then deploy an orbital core to enter your robot.

Move & look
Move with WASD / arrow keys or the touch pad. Mouse movement aims without moving your scout. Middle-drag pans on desktop; touch-drag pans on phones. + / - zoom; C recenters.
Deploy
Open Ground forces (B), choose APCR sentry, tap clear ground, then confirm. Escape cancels. Use Site controls → coordinates for keyboard placement. Only you can dismantle your sentries.
Robot weapons
After deploying a core: hold LMB / Space for the fuel jet, RMB / J for the laser rifle. Phones have HOLD FUEL and HOLD LASER buttons. Your robot faces its aim while WASD stays in world directions. Fuel travels up to five tiles and kills on direct contact. Laser pulses instantly reach the first enemy or obstacle, up to 32 tiles. Survivors burn and flee for 2.4 seconds, then resume attacking. Rocks and solid structures stop both weapons without friendly damage; floor panels do not. The personal fuel jet leaves no ground fire.
Sentry modes
Inspect your sentry through Site controls and choose Machine gun or Flamethrower. Heavy fuel reaches eight tiles, farther than the robot jet. It kills on direct contact and stops at rocks or solid structures. Your choice is saved.
Rover weapon choices
The Cannon selector chooses HE or Magnetic. Magnetic takes one second to charge, then fires a fast cyan-white streak along the current turret bearing, up to 24 tiles. It hits the first enemy or obstacle without an explosion or crater; reload takes 2.5 seconds after firing. Click once to begin charging. Changing weapons, opening menus or losing focus cancels charging. The RMB selector keeps Machine gun and adds Fuel jet, using the same eight-tile stream as the sentry. Hold RMB / J or the labelled phone button for the selected secondary. These controls belong to the starting rover and any tank you board. Deploying your core changes you into a robot; call a Tank drop pod from Ground forces to get a separate vehicle.
Default rover weapons
Left-click fires the cannon; hold right-click for machine gun. The LMB/RMB panel only shows weapon activity. On phones, tap to aim and use CANNON or hold MG. With the map focused, Space fires HE and J holds MG; site coordinates also set a keyboard aim point. HE shells detonate in a 2.1-tile radius, strongest at the center. The gold crosshair is your desired target. The pale-blue diamond and dashed blast ring follow the actual turret, which turns at 180 degrees per second independently of the hull. Both weapons fire along the barrel while it turns. The ring previews the unobstructed HE landing point; rocks or enemies can stop a shot sooner. Only enemies take damage; rocks stop the projectile but the blast can reach around them. Range is 14 tiles. Machine-gun and sentry APCR rounds hit one enemy, stop at rocks, and create no blast or crater. Automatic sentries turn toward a target at 270 degrees per second and wait until aligned before firing. Armor/penetration mechanics are not included yet.
Coastal gunship
After deploying your core, choose Navy in the deployment menu. Drop the ship into clear coastal water; the preview marks its shore dock. Approach that dock and press E to board. W/S moves forward/backward, A/D steers, mouse aims the independent turret, one LMB click or Space press fires one ready twin-barrel turret; two rapid clicks or presses fire all ready main turrets, and 1/2/3 selects HE/Fire/Gas. Hold RMB/J or the Side guns button for close defence: port and starboard mounts each cover a 160-degree outward arc, up to eight tiles. Green sectors show an eligible side; steer to bring bow or stern targets into an arc. Main range is 6 to 40 tiles. Each turret reloads independently for six seconds, shared across its shell types. FORE/AFT readouts show readiness. Return to a marked berth, align with its arrow, and press E to disembark. Show dock locates your berth. Ship, hull damage, seat, ammunition and reload are saved. Firing can attract three swimming aliens. Naval contact requests a test encounter; contact groups share a 30-second timer and six-enemy cap. Attack circles warn before a strike: sail away to dodge. If destroyed, recover your pilot at a friendly core, then use Navy / Replace destroyed ship after the cargo timer. Keep the original delivery site clear. Sail within two tiles of clear shore and press G / Shore ramp to anchor and deploy a boarding ramp. E disembarks; return to the ramp to board again. G retracts it before sailing. The ramp and landing position are saved. One robot occupies the ship; passenger seats are not implemented.
Bomber flight test
From your robot, choose Air force / Airbase + bomber. Place the 6-by-10 facility on clear land at least 16 tiles inside the map. Approach the gold boarding apron and press E to board and take off. W accelerates, S slows, A/D turn; the aircraft always flies forward at 6 to 12 tiles per second. Faster flight means wider turns. Mouse position does not steer or aim bombs. Gold circles predict six HE impacts; LMB or Drop 6 HE releases a run and holds course for 1.25 seconds. Two runs per load. Slow to 7 tiles per second or less and return inside your base's green recovery circle for automatic landing. Rearming takes eight seconds; Take off starts another sortie, or E disembarks. The map boundary turns the plane inward automatically. Flights and ground service pause with no viewers; disconnected pilots retain their seat while the plane cruises if anyone remains. Aircraft, ammunition and base persist; bombs in flight clear on restart. This first flight test has no fuel or anti-air damage yet.
Missile submarine
Choose Navy / Missile submarine. It uses the same dock and G shore ramp as the gunship when surfaced. WASD sails. Aim with the mouse or a map tap; R rotates the 6-by-8 strike area. The whole area must lie 12 to 80 tiles away. LMB / Launch 6 commits six missiles; 1/2/3 selects HE/Fire/Gas. The hull braces through the 1.25-second launch sequence, then can sail while missiles travel. Reload takes 18 seconds after the last launch. Left Ctrl dives; Space surfaces. Phone buttons provide both controls. Each transition takes two seconds and remains vulnerable. Fully submerged, swimmers lose track; WASD still sails. Thirty seconds of dive battery forces surfacing at zero; surface recharge is two battery seconds per second. At least ten seconds of battery is needed to dive. Missiles, ramps, boarding and exiting require a fully surfaced hull. Retract the ramp and finish launching before diving. Docking grants no immunity: place APCR or flamethrower sentries on nearby shore to defend the berth, keeping its boarding tile clear. There are no side guns. One gunship and one submarine per scout, with four vessels total per world. Use Selected vessel in Navy to find or replace either ship.
Tank drop pod
After deploying your core, open Ground forces and choose Tank drop pod. Select a land site for its 1-tile center and four 1-by-4 panels. The pod lands, unfolds and releases a tank. Approach within 1.8 tiles and press E or Board tank. Your robot rides inside. WASD drives, LMB fires the selected cannon and RMB holds the selected secondary. E or Exit tank returns your robot to clear ground beside it. Tank position, equipment and your seat are saved. Exit before leaving if a teammate should drive; disconnected drivers keep their seat. One tank per scout. Crawlers can damage and destroy tanks. Recover your pilot at a friendly core, then choose Replace destroyed tank after the cargo timer. Find my deployed tank centers the camera.
Orbital core
Open Ground forces and choose Orbital core. Inspect the cross-shaped footprint, then confirm a land site. The pod lands, four metal panels unfold, and your robot exits on the right. Covered rocks become walkable flooring. Each scout has one saved core deployment in this field test; resource costs come later. Sound is not included yet.
Survival & recovery
Robots have 100 hull health. Crawlers mark an orange strike circle before hitting for 25 damage: move out to dodge. Burning interrupts the attack. At zero health, movement, fire and construction stop. After three seconds, select any completed friendly core and deploy a replacement. The circle marks its recovery area. The pod takes 1.8 seconds, restores health and grants a two-second landing shield. It brings no new core or sentry. Health and recovery survive reload and restart. If a landing becomes blocked, choose a clear core and try again.
Railway cannon
Lay straight rails from Ground forces, then deploy a 3 by 3 cannon on the middle of three connected tiles. Select it or choose Operate a railway cannon. One teammate controls it at a time; your robot holds position and guards with fuel nearby and laser at distance. It remains vulnerable. The orbital overview opens automatically; pan and zoom freely. Mouse or touch sets the gold desired target; the blue diamond and ring show the actual landing point as the cannon traverses and elevates. WASD or the touch pad travels along rails: A/D on east-west track, W/S on north-south. A bend completes before travel along its new axis. Use 1/2/3 or the shell buttons for HE/Fire/Gas. Wait for stabilizers, then press Fire HE, LMB on the map, or Space with the map focused. Maximum range is 60 tiles at 45 degrees; raising above 45 degrees shortens range and steepens descent. The ring is the predicted landing point. The expanded map is 128 by 96; off-map shots are rejected. Reload takes six seconds. The lob passes over obstacles. Its aiming circle shows a three-tile blast radius; the separate eight-tile crater is cosmetic. Escape returns to your body. Rails and carriage progress are saved. Choose one-tile corners or a junction in Rail track. Laying north-south rails over your east-west rails makes a junction automatically. Drive to its tile, then use W/S for the north/south branch or A/D for the east/west branch. Adjacent rails must connect edge to edge. The cannon remains 3 by 3 and needs physical clearance while turning. Existing broad curves remain usable. Choose HE, Incendiary or Gas in the bottom cannon manual. Fire kills on contact for 18 seconds; enemies avoid its three-tile core and slow in the outer heat band. Gas lures enemies within ten tiles and holds six kills in a 2.6-tile cloud, with no timer. Fire avoidance overrides gas attraction. Fields never harm friendlies. Maximum 12 fields, eight of one type, including incoming shells. Fields survive drill changes but reset on server restart. All shell types share a six-second reload. Upgrade your own curve through Site controls with Install junction switch. Connect its straight continuation and both curve approaches first. Rail junction switches in Ground forces, or Switches in the cannon manual, selects straight or curved travel. The entire apron locks while any cannon overlaps it; choose the route before entering. Route changes are shared and saved.
Missile battery
Deploy a fixed 4 by 4 battery from Ground forces. The selected tile is its top-left corner. Take control, aim the 12 by 18 target rectangle in orbital view, and use LMB, Space or Launch 12. R or Rotate area swaps its long axis. The whole area must fit inside the map and 15 to 100 tiles from the battery. Choose 1/2/3 or HE/Fire/Gas. Twelve missiles launch at quarter-second intervals, climb, cruise and dive into separate target sectors. Reload takes 15 seconds after the last launch. Impacts are committed when fired; changing aim or releasing control cannot redirect them. Two volleys may fly at once. HE leaves overlapping craters. Fire ignites each landed sector for 18 seconds; enemies avoid it and slow near the heat. Gas lures nearby monsters into landed sectors and shares 18 kills across the whole strike, with no timer. Only enemies take damage. Large fields share an area budget with cannon fields; a rejected launch spends no reload. Position and reload are saved; volleys reset on server restart and pause when nobody is connected.
Combat drill
Choose Caldera landing, Eastern plain or Southern reach, then start a three-crawler drill in that sector. The causeway links the original island to the eastern mainland. Defend it together. Drills pause when nobody is connected and reset on server restart. Construction and scout positions remain saved.
Return
Keep this browser's cookies to keep your guest identity. Buildings save immediately; movement saves every half second.
Visual effects

Reduced effects also disables camera shake. Damage is identical in both modes. Audio is coming later.
