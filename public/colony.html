    MYCORRHIZAL / FIELD NOTES

    A little guidance

    WASD / arrows
    Move your robot.
    Drag / wheel
    Pan and zoom the view.
    Walk near a bed
    See its plant or seed choices. Click a bed to inspect from farther away.
    E
    Water or gather the displayed crop.
    B · C
    Build a bed · find your robot.
    Esc
    Close a panel or cancel placement; otherwise open the expedition menu.

    Seeds and water are free. Dry crops pause; ripe crops wait.

    Crops condition the ground around their bed. Build only on conditioned ground; ground nothing tends slowly returns to wild. Your starting clearing never does.

    Furnish opens your starter collection and settlement projects. Select a piece and a clear site; a shuttle lowers its cargo and assembles it. Click a finished furnishing to move, rotate or store it. R rotates a placement.

    Explore takes your robot to two regions beyond the home district. Walk near a marked discovery and press E or Record. Your buildings stay in the home district, and your garden keeps growing. Use Return home or Home in the toolbar to go back. Esc closes field notes without leaving the region.

    Fish from the lake shore or a pond: press E to cast, wait for the bite, then E again to reel. Catches fill the creel in Camp and are treats for your companion. Adopt the furry mechanical spider in Camp; it follows you, sits beside you when you rest, and E pets it.

    Walk up to a bench, rest pad, charging post or kettle and press E to sit, charge or brew. Any movement stands you up. Resting changes nothing in the garden; it is a moment of your own.

    Your shuttle unloads its reward as crates beside the pad. Walk up to one and press E to collect it, or build a warehouse: its drone carries every crate home and the yard shows what you have.

    Crew members share the garden, supplies and harvest.

    Industry turns crops into provisions and biomass. Power starts charged: add a harvest outlet beside a bed, a processor on its OUT side and a generator for the biomass, and the chain feeds itself. Each card has one Load button that picks the best cargo; the processor cooks any crop. Arrows show flow and R turns equipment. Belts can enter the orbital post to store cargo. The orbital post sends everything in the reserve by default: each crop earns 8 materials and each provision 12.

    Camp places free bonfires and an outdoor screen. The host can choose shared light and weather under Atmosphere. Film sound is personal; use Sound on to listen.
